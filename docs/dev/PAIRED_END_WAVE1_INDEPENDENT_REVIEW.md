# Independent review: wave 1 scan-stage plan

2026-10-16 · Baseline `41899ae4b8209893f7b5ae0cf893304e6796dabf` · Design review only

## 1. Verdict

**Do not implement the plan as written.** Complete pairs, overlap eligibility, per-candidate emissions and the annotation-supported acceptance witness remain sound objectives.
Seven blockers remain: local likelihood merging; the prior's population denominator; mismatch-based proposal exclusion; validation before reconstruction; incomplete reconstruction rules; unsupported splice paths/blacklist bypasses; and unknown sequence evidence.
The three central arguments are respectively **refuted**, **refuted as a completeness claim**, and **partly confirmed but incorrectly restricted**.
The owner's unannotated-splice policy and two-pass approximation are retained; their costs must be measured without treating provenance as truth.
Resolve the local scoring dependencies before activating alternatives. Remote-copy search and the general downstream redesign can retain their later waves.
No production files, original plans, or goldens were changed.

The plan was read first and completely. This review also uses the roadmap, revision 2, first review and evidence, restart handoff, splice-artifact specification, and the named production sources. [Executable audit](paired_end_wave1_review_evidence/audit.py) and [results](paired_end_wave1_review_evidence/audit.json) contain new finite calculations and baseline native probes. Library measurements below are recorded evidence, not new BAM runs. Coordinates are zero-based, half-open; junction ends are named low/high, following `DESIGN.md` §0.

## 2. Findings, most severe first

**1 — blocker · §§10.2, 8.5: the local merger is not an emission-preserving combination.** *Derived; measured on the baseline scorer.* `score_mm_alignment` selects lower overhang **before** likelihood. For two overlapping mates, a contiguous reading can have overhang 1 and NM 0, while its spliced alternative has overhang 0 and NM 2. With the shipped penalties and equal length/strand factors, their RNA terms are **0.1 and 0.01**; the merger retains **0.01**. The overlap/error model permits the former, so “the transcript's own path” does not justify discarding it. Also, multi-junction local reconstruction can yield more than two contiguous origins: the claimed factor-2 bound fails. [Scorer](../../src/rigel/native/scoring.cpp:474).

Specify canonical physical origins and their weights for local readings, preserving each emission. Distinct origins require marginalization with corresponding opportunity/prior; duplicate representations contribute once. Merely replacing overhang-first with maximum likelihood would still be an approximation, and merely changing mean to sum would leave normalization unspecified. **Test:** the finite cases in §3, duplicated reports, negligible extra origins, and unequal length/strand factors; compare with enumerated normalized origin probabilities.

**2 — blocker · §10.2, “The prior”: pass-1 holding does not mean exclusion from calibration.** *Derived; measured function probe.* `_drain_side_buffer` deposits held placements **before** calibration. `n_calibrated` nevertheless excludes every multimapper; the source explicitly documents this mismatch. With calibrated gDNA count 6, 10 unique units and 10 held-and-deposited units, the intended cohort share is **6/20 = 0.3**, but the current denominator gives **6/10 = 0.6**. For 20 gDNA-eligible units, the pseudocounts are **12/8 instead of 6/14**. With zero unique units, they become **0/0**, even with positive calibrated gDNA. [Prior function](../../src/rigel/pipeline.py:686).

Define a matched numerator/denominator cohort, including how drawn local placements enter it. This is an accounting dependency of newly created local multimappers, separate from accepting an approximate draw. **Test:** preserve deposits while changing only local-reading multiplicity; verify cohort counts, prior odds and the all-local-multimapper limit. If correction remains deferred, §10.2 and the wave-end correctness claim must acknowledge the unresolved dependency.

**3 — blocker · §§8.3–8.7: Δm ≤ 0 changes support according to the reported parent.** *Derived.* With `NM(S)=0`, `NM(C)=1`, a reported C produces `{C,S}`, but reported S produces `{S}`. Deduplicating final alignments cannot recover an alternative never generated. One sequencing error at a locally discriminating base suffices; remote origin is unnecessary. Remaining likelihood factors can outweigh one mismatch, so proposal rejection does decide some competitions. The recorded reference-tie percentages do not bound this error.

Enumerate the finite, structurally allowed local readings independently of mismatch preference; let the existing emission score them. Any pruning needs a justified bound on the **complete** score, not a new Δm cutoff. **Test:** switch the reported parent for identical bases, using Δm = −1, 0, +1 and overlapping mates; require identical reachable readings and scores. Measure true-origin recall and RNA loss, including sample/reference differences.

**4 — blocker · §§4, 7–8: validating the original placement can suppress a valid edited one.** *Derived; independently enumerated.* Take annotated junction `[1200,2100)`, F `[1103,1203)`, R `[2150,2250)`, with a three-base sequence tie. The original path has length **1,147**, exceeding the existing limit 1,000. The annotation intron is not wholly inside the unaligned gap `[1203,2150)`, so §7 cannot remove it. Moving F's final three bases across the junction gives `97M900N3M@1103`, length **247**, and valid inward containment.

Perform only sound preliminary rejection before reconstruction; validate containment and length on each **final** reading. Do not erase aligned bases in the original witness to evade this case. **Test:** this example, its reverse-strand mirror, and a corrected reading that becomes too long. Restoring early length rejection must fail the test.

**5 — blocker · §§8.1–8.5, 12: reconstruction is neither complete nor fully defined.** *Derived.* A mate spanning a skip does not inherently require deleting internal bases: moving its entire suffix can construct S (§3). The owner's reconciliation example also admits a second contiguous anchoring direction omitted from its expected table. Overlapping unequal skips, a block wholly inside another mate's skip, interacting edits and indels need actual query mappings; “recompute NM” does not specify those mappings.

Define the finite allowed anchors and paths, map every retained query index exactly once, preserve clips, and validate the complete pair. Resolve edits jointly, including any larger suffix required by another edit; do not combine coordinate deltas on stale maps. Recompute all geometry-dependent emissions: moving an end can change length, coverage and candidate membership as well as overhang/NM. **Test:** exhaustive base maps for these cases, both anchoring directions, shared edited query positions, and edit-order permutations. Record an explicit unresolved reason when no construction exists.

**6 — blocker · §§9, 10–12: individual annotation and blacklist removal are not path admissibility.** *Derived; measured resolver/scorer probes.* Two junctions can each be annotated while no transcript contains both. The audit's same-gene pair has exactly that property; baseline `merge_sets` falls back to union and returns **TJ1: 150/50/500** and **TJ2: 150/50/250**, classified annotated spliced. Both lengths are numeric, but neither transcript supplies the observed chain. Thus the break test “withhold a reading with only annotated junctions” is wrong. [Resolver](../../src/rigel/native/resolve_context.h:1185), [set merge](../../src/rigel/native/constants.h:278).

Separately, the blacklist removes N evidence without moving the blocks. `SPLICE_ARTIFACT` is then gDNA-capable, which the native probe confirms. A read `10M20N10M` cannot become a contiguous 20-base read at unchanged coordinates merely by forgetting N. This also lets a blacklisted novel junction evade a policy checked only on retained junctions. [Parsing](../../src/rigel/native/bam_scanner.cpp:663), [gDNA eligibility](../../src/rigel/native/scoring.cpp:226).

Require joint observed-splice compatibility separately from overlap eligibility; never impose transcript endpoint containment. Preserve raw junctions through policy checks. Retain blacklist provenance, but do not certify contiguity without a constructed mapping. Any changed blacklist behavior needs its own matched comparison. **Test:** individually known but jointly unsupported junctions; mixed known/novel chains; fully/partly blacklisted chains; an independent valid alternative surviving beside each failure.

**7 — blocker · §§6, 8.1, 8.6–8.7: K cannot supply missing mismatch evidence.** *Derived by non-identifiability.* With `SEQ=*`, `2M`, old reference `AA` and NM 1, the unseen read could be `AC` or `CA`. Moving its last base to reference C gives final NM **0 or 2**, respectively. Both have identical available evidence and a one-base edit. The promised exact recomputation and parent independence cannot hold by choosing K.

Recover sequence before filtering carrier records. When an edit's Δm is provable without sequence—for example identical unambiguous old/new reference strings—use that proof. Otherwise retain “unknown” explicitly and withhold the unscorable edit under the owner's rule, unless a separately derived marginal emission is adopted. Likewise define the disposition for missing/conflicting original NM that cannot be recomputed. **Test:** the two indistinguishable inputs above, partial hard-clipped recovery, and an exact reference tie; never substitute NM 0 or copy stale NM onto a changed map.

**8 — fix before implementing · §§5–6, 8.7, 10.3: identity must retain path and score information.** *Derived.* `50M3D50M` and `50M3N50M` map every aligned query base to the same reference base and have identical clips, but differ in path and NM. §6's stated equivalence would collapse them. Include operation semantics, query coordinates, raw skips and evidence state in canonical identity. Check verified STAR NH/HI completeness on raw per-mate records before filtering or deduplication. After adding NM to the deferred bank, update copying, sorting, export/import and `lift_choices`; identical geometry no longer implies identical scoring records. [Deferred schema](../../src/rigel/native/calibration/accumulator.h:305).

**Test:** D/N distinction, shifted homopolymer indels, record permutations, opposite-orientation sequence carriers, spill/reload, and equal geometry with unequal NM. Apply one declared NM convention; SAM counts I/D and ambiguous symbols but excludes N/S/H, and specially handles SEQ `=`. Reverse-aligned SEQ is already reverse-complemented. [SAM tags](https://samtools.github.io/hts-specs/SAMtags.pdf), [SAM format](https://samtools.github.io/hts-specs/SAMv1.pdf). Check reference names/lengths and canonical M5 calculation; absent M5 leaves identity unverified, not proven.

**9 — fix before implementing · §§10.3, 13: three calibration changes are three mechanisms.** *Derived; measured arithmetic.* The length and sequence corrections have the right intent. The density correction needs normalization, not simply replacing an inapplicable zero by 1: otherwise a placement without a gap gets one density factor while another gets two. The audit changes their probabilities **0.5/0.5 → 1/11,10/11** by multiplying all actual densities by ten, with relative support unchanged. Details and the accepted approximation are in §5.

**Test:** density-unit invariance, observed/implied-splice length-law selection, NM-only odds, hard-zero penalties and stable log-space normalization. Run each correction as its own A/B, with mismatch support in place before active unequal-NM alternatives. The final combined arm does not replace isolated comparisons.

**10 — fix before implementing · §§7, 8.5, 11–13: make limits and accounting executable.** *Derived.* “No cap” meets a `uint16` hit count cast: **65,536 becomes 0; 65,537 becomes 1**. Widen or check representations; never silently truncate hypotheses. The ledger also needs unresolved-sequence/path reasons and explicit priority when annotation failure and length failure coexist. State whether `include_multimap=false` excludes formerly unique templates gaining local alternatives, and measure that cost. [Buffer append](../../src/rigel/native/resolve_context.h:247).

**Test:** width boundaries, two reasons on different combinations, unused record aliases, all-filtered groups, and stale output tags. Preserve the continuous clip witness too: F `8S2M@108`, R `2M8S@100` describe a valid **10-base contiguous** molecule, contradicting §7's claim that apparent outward anchors require a hidden junction. Performance optimization can follow correctness: compare exact enumeration against any deduplicated/shared representation, including adversarial multi-junction inputs and peak memory.

## 3. The three arguments, with finite examples

**Existing combination (§10.2): refuted generally; confirmed only in a restricted case.** Let both mates cover `[1101,1201)` in C, or `[1101,1200)+[1500,1501)` in S. Both read the same A at the moved position; the alternative reference base is C. Both readings have molecule length 100. C has one union-overhang base and zero mismatches; S has zero overhang and two sequenced mismatches. Starting from reported S, §8.3 generates C because Δm = −2. The native merger nevertheless selects S's **−4.605170** RNA log term over C's **−2.302585**. With the supported `mismatch_alpha=0` setting, the same probe emits **zero EM units**, despite C's valid RNA and gDNA terms. This is a local pair, not a remote-placement objection.

One **genic** contiguous hit does contribute its own gDNA term; adding S does not enter its denominator. But `10M10N10M10N10M@100`, paired with reverse `[150,180)`, permits contiguous F starts **100, 110, 120** by retaining each anchor in turn. On matching sequence, all three are distinct origins. With equal per-origin terms 0.5, the current mean is **0.5**, versus numerator sum **1.5**: factor three, not two. Proper normalization must also account for their origin opportunities. Reconciliation admits unequal-NM alternatives, so multiple C readings do not require both sides to tie.

**Δm ≤ 0 (§8.6): refuted as a sound completeness boundary.** At one discriminating position let contiguous reference be A, spliced reference C, and observed read C. A genuine local gDNA A→C sequencing error yields `NM(C)=1, NM(S)=0`. Report S and C disappears; report C and both remain. Under a symmetric substitution process this event has probability **ε/3 per discriminating site**; a sample variant can produce the same observation without sequencing error. No frequency estimate is needed to prove the gap. The recorded **996/1,056** remote fits leave **60 nonremote fits (5.68%)** in that selected cohort; neither those numbers nor reference distance h measure the complete-pair cost of this Δm rule. STAR's junction score bonus is real, but does not make the rule complete. [STAR parameter definitions](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/alexdobin/STAR/2.7.11b/source/parametersDefault).

**Reconciliation without invented bases (§8.2): confirmed for explicit maps, refuted as “S impossible for spanning mates.”** For F `50M3N50M@1000` and R `[1040,1140)`, construct R as **`10M3N90M@1040`**. Its first ten query bases stay; its remaining ninety move +3. No query base disappears or appears. With F unchanged, the common spliced molecule is `[1000,1050)+[1053,1143)`, **L = 140**. Sequence may favor or disfavor it; construction alone decides neither. Annotate the three-base junction in this dedicated fixture: it is absent from the plan's toy annotation, where §9 would forbid generating C as well.

The owner's larger example constructs its listed S and low-anchored C correctly. It also allows high-anchored C: move the spliced mate's first 50 bases `[1150,1200)` to `[1450,1500)`, giving mates `[1103,1203)` and `[1450,1550)`, genomic **L = 447**. The native resolver returns TA1 **147/53/147**, TA2 **97/103/447**, and the synthetic gene-span RNA transcript **200/0/447**. Omitting this reading needs a stated restriction and measured cost, not a claim of nonconstructibility.

## 4. Unannotated splicing: policy and measured cost

Retain the owner's rule: a reading with a novel observed junction is withheld, with no alternative generated by editing that junction. An independently reported valid reading of the same template survives. This deliberately loses some genuine unannotated RNA and some recoverable gDNA artifacts; it is an annotation/search policy, not a physical impossibility theorem. The baseline already computes numerical lengths for many such readings, so “cannot compute” should mean **no admissible annotated RNA path**, not unavailable arithmetic.

The [recorded VCaP screen](SPLICE_ARTIFACTS_DESIGN.md:76) used unique primary alignments with XS, one unit per **template–junction**, not one complete validated template:

| Recorded quantity | Recomputed consequence |
|---|---|
| 114,519 unannotated RNA-library units / 14,926,264 RNA-library junction units | **0.7672% exposure** to withholding |
| 2,253 unannotated DNA-library units / 6,517 DNA-library artifact units | **34.5711%** of artifact units in that screen |
| 114,519 / 2,253 | **50.83 RNA-source units per DNA-source unit** |

RNA-library provenance does not prove genuine RNA, and junction-unit exposure is not transcript loss or final template withholding. The quoted minimap2 **90% unannotated long-anchor artifact composition** likewise supplies neither genuine-RNA cost nor post-pairing recall. Those costs remain unmeasured for the proposed complete-pair policy.

Recount after validation and blacklist handling, deduplicated by template, recording whether another usable reading survives. Report truth-weighted RNA mass lost, artifact RNA mass removed, transcript/gene error and calibration changes per stratum. Include annotation-withholding controls and novel combinations of known sites. The three reporting classes need deterministic gene-set rules for nested/overlapping genes, multiple junctions and intergenic flanks; span-based “read-through” is a reporting convention, not demonstrated biology. Record all junction classes before choosing one template reason.

## 5. Calibration: corrections and the accepted approximation

**Length law: yes, conditionally.** An admissible reading with an observed annotated splice is RNA even when it has no implied gap intron. Select the RNA law for observed **or** implied splicing; the current branch tests only implied introns. Use the final reading's annotation compatibility, including unknown motif handling, rather than a stale parent flag. Contiguous readings retain the declared mixed/global law; they are not automatically gDNA. [Second-pass scoring](../../src/rigel/second_pass.py:548).

**Density factor: right diagnosis, incomplete correction.** A placement with no contested gap has no within-placement choice. However, multiplying its abundance by 1 while multiplying another abundance by an unnormalized gap density compares quantities with different density powers. Derive a dimensionless within-placement choice weight—e.g. explicitly normalized conditional hypothesis support—and combine that with one placement abundance and the length/strand/sequence terms. Specify all-zero support and multiple-gap normalization, then test scale invariance and replicated candidate descriptions. This is more than a sentinel-value edit.

**Sequence factor: yes, once per reading.** Carry final pair NM and the configured `mismatch_alpha`, including settings 0 and 1; do not introduce a different penalty or apply Δm again. Conditional on equal other factors, NM difference d gives odds **α_mm^d**; at the default, difference 37 gives **10⁻³⁷**. Use stable arithmetic and preserve explicit hard gates. Add NM to every bank/key/copy/serialization path, including oracle choice lifting. This corrects sequence weighting in pass 2; it does not make calibration and EM's complete models identical. Implement it in a separate measured arm before unequal-NM readings become active.

**All-held: accept as instructed, measure its effect.** The plan's “all become contiguous” result additionally requires positive competing C support and compatible earlier factors. Baseline `combine_factors` gives **S/C = 0/1** for placement support `[0,1]`, but **0.5/0.5** for `[0,0]` when other factors tie: a wholly zero factor is dropped. A C draw is a contiguous deposit, not a predetermined gDNA assignment. Inflation of the fitted gDNA prior depends on subsequent calibration and the cohort defect in finding 2. [Factor combination](../../src/rigel/second_pass.py:164).

Record unavailable support, partial hard zeros, predicted draw probabilities, actual draws and truth separately. Compare oracle reading deposits with actual deposits, then calibrated component counts, RNA length/strand training, locus priors and final RNA loss. Use matched seeds for A/B and additional seeds to price sampling variation. Include all-held, partly held, independently supported and wholly zero-support loci at different depths. A zero-weight count alone cannot establish acceptable accuracy. This accepted approximation is not reopened as a demand for joint calibration/EM fitting.

## 7. Open questions (§14): recommendations

**1. Δm ≤ 0 and Δm = 1.** The mechanism argument is insufficient. Include structurally valid local alternatives regardless of mismatch preference, then score them with the retained emission. Adding only Δm = 1 moves the arbitrary exclusion to 2 and breaks the no-new-cut rule. Measure misses by true origin, mismatch difference, local/remote placement and both-mate completeness; genome-only realignment is a recall instrument, not a truth oracle. Examine missed origins even when some other C reading already exists.

**2. K with unavailable bases.** K bounds an edit's size, not its evidence. Use recovered bases or a provable Δm; withhold unscorable edits and count them. Preserve original valid readings where possible. State how this proposal-only use relates to the resolver's existing K-based gap logic, so an ostensibly local change does not silently alter candidate emissions.

**3. Unannotated withholding and blacklist.** Keep the owner's policy, including its loss of genuine novel RNA. Apply it to raw observed junctions and jointly unsupported chains, with independent valid alternatives preserved. Resolve the blacklist conflict before coding: unchanged early deletion cannot also guarantee physically complete gDNA readings. Compare any changed treatment in its own two-error arm; no blanket blacklist retirement is justified here.

**4. Existing EM combination and factor two.** Reject the general claim. One genic C term is correct in isolation, but the RNA ordering, multiple local origins and prior cohort each fail finite checks. Pull the minimal local normalization/accounting dependency forward, or keep generated readings diagnostic-only until it is solved. Neither option requires implementing remote-copy search now.

**5. All-held approximation and correction order.** Retain the accepted two-pass approximation, with the conditional behavior and downstream measurements in §5. Establish length selection, normalized density applicability and final-NM transport separately before active comparisons. Introduce mismatch weighting before unequal-NM readings; add each mechanism in its own arm and then test their interaction. Do not present the accepted approximation as approval of the separate prior-denominator defect.

**6. Exhaustive enumeration.** Keep an exhaustive finite reference. Bound the search structurally by defined local anchors, annotation paths, fixed query observations and the existing length limit; canonicalize final maps. Avoid repeated edit cycles and silently capped results. Measure generated, rejected and distinct readings, count-width safety, time and peak memory. Optimize only with parity against the reference; resource exhaustion must be explicit, never an unreported change of hypotheses.

**Before implementation:** revise the plan around (1) lossless record normalization and raw policy evidence; (2) preliminary reference/orientation checks; (3) coherent local reading construction; (4) final mapping/geometry/length and joint-splice validation; (5) canonical deduplication with recomputed emissions; (6) reviewed local scoring/prior and calibration contracts; (7) the template/record ledger. **At wave 1 completion:** all reference, record-level, mutation, persistence and full-suite checks must pass; oracle identity exceptions must be enumerated by template. Run each mechanism separately with `scan.total_threads=1`, `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1`, fractional EM and recorded seeds. Split the three calibration corrections into separate arms. Report artifact RNA, genuine RNA loss, transcript/gene error, calibration/prior errors and conservation per required stratum, with unstranded ON separately.

Recount VCaP transitions on complete pairs and use simulator truth for accuracy. The existing evidence does not establish joint law-and-ruler-frozen performance for the new plan.
