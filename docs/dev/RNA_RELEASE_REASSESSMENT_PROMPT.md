# Prompt for an independent Rigel 0.8.0 review

You are an independent scientific and implementation reviewer for Rigel, an RNA transcript quantifier that separates genomic DNA contamination. We need a candid reassessment of the model and code and a **simple, elegant, shippable path to 0.8.0**. Do not assume the current shared-capture-field direction is correct or that more sophisticated inference is the answer. Recommend deletion, a narrower repair, or selective rollback if the evidence supports it.

Read `docs/dev/RNA_RELEASE_REASSESSMENT_REVIEW.md` first. It is the current synthesis and includes numbers, source links, qualifications, and competing explanations. Then read `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/SUCCESS.md` for scope and measurement contracts. Use the review's code map to inspect production and experimental implementations. Consult `SHARED_CAPTURE_FIELD_DESIGN.md`, `SHARED_CAPTURE_FIELD_REVIEW.md`, and `SHARED_CAPTURE_FIELD_IMPLEMENTATION.md` for derivations and failures. The original capture brief and expectation plan preserve earlier assumptions; their diagnoses were partly refuted. Do not treat chronological “next steps” as settled decisions.

A source/evidence bundle accompanies the review. Its `HANDOFF.md` explains the layout, omitted data, and hashes. If you have only the prose, state that limitation and request the specific source/evidence needed; do not claim to have reviewed code or reproduced measurements you cannot access.

The important facts:

- Released `v0.7.1` is commit `ff26d8e5`; main is `624dfade`. The experimental native shared-field code is outside main and unselected. Main's standing suite is 2,837 passing tests; that does not validate the prototype. The real tagged October 5 benchmark exists. It shows useful strengths and substantial false-gDNA/synthetic-RNA errors. Historical `current_cli` and September 30 arms are not today's main or the release tag.
- Required release strata are stranded capture-OFF, stranded capture-ON, and unstranded capture-OFF. Unstranded capture-ON is deferred but always reported. Fragment-length gaps in both directions are required. Capture is a local continuous spectrum, not a production ON/OFF classification.
- Main's ladder g00 stranded-OFF transcript error falls **13.61→1.93%** when capture weights are forced to one. Silent-isoform mass falls 251,216→17,058. This attributes the regression; it does not validate flat production weights. Harmful weights include factor-driven posterior mode switches at substantial depths, not merely shallow unbiased noise.
- Posterior mean readouts, local coupling, and own-likelihood readers have already failed wider comparisons. One reader improves ladder averages but worsens RNA-short stranded-ON **14.24→20.43%**. Demand all rungs and both gap directions.
- Deferred unstranded capture-ON misallocates captured exonic DNA to synthetic unspliced RNA after removal of an intron constraint. Calibration has **no direct length-composition likelihood**, despite an earlier brief claiming otherwise. Exon/intron totals alone cannot identify DNA versus RNA when the other channels are blind.
- The proposed mechanism uses one latent field per locus, candidate-specific conserved path shares, and posterior sampling with auxiliary variables collapsed out. Each fragment enters one mixture likelihood. Its numerator does **not** universally cancel between components. Changing observed length measures, geometry, candidate numerators, and VB to MAP accompanied the implementation, so gains over shipped cannot automatically be attributed to field integration.
- The latest shared-field **test-chromosome** g00 transcript errors are 10.95/14.28/14.91/14.65%, versus shipped 7.66/8.22/5.62/9.40% in str-OFF/str-ON/unstr-OFF/deferred-ON order. Rate and independent free-junction variants also fail. These are finite-budget screens, not converged model optima. On g50, new geometry with the **fixed reader field** beats the integrated arm on all four transcript rows.
- Enumeration and numerical gates corrected real mistakes: an inconsistent hybrid objective, a failing second-order expansion, share representation, and unstable length arithmetic. They have not established a learnable physical capture model, reliable large-locus optimization, or release improvement. There is no completed new-field ladder/both-gap/VCaP accuracy matrix.

Focus your review on these decisions:

1. What is the correct estimand and likelihood after conditioning on capture, observed fragment length, mapping, and annotation? Can the conserved-share field represent the relevant selection process, or is it an accounting model being mistaken for capture physics? Show a minimal derivation or counterexample.
2. What distinguishes expression, gDNA composition, and capture when gDNA is absent or scarce? Which claims are identifiable from likelihood, and which are supplied by priors, annotation assumptions, or reused evidence? Could conditioning on matched footprints/length remove nuisance parameters and yield a simpler model?
3. Which regressions are model defects, which are inference defects, and which are code defects? Audit numerators and normalizers together; examine junctions, missing positions, partial locus memberships, grouped priors, and the VB/MAP change. Do not prescribe a larger sampler until the objective is worth optimizing.
4. Is a minimal candidate-numerator/normalizer repair, a calibrated local reader repair, or selective restoration of an older mechanism more promising than shared-field integration? State one discriminating experiment for each serious option. Do not blindly restore a removed heuristic or repackage a failed average.
5. What implementation can be deleted? Propose the smallest production boundary with explicit inputs, outputs, assumptions, and necessary tests. A framework, more knobs, or a long risk catalogue is not the requested result.

Respect fixed constraints: **no detector, no library-level reference, no new empirical constant, no tuning to panels**. Biological populations are gDNA/RNA+/RNA−; “nascent” is an annotation/robustness device. If a constraint prevents a stated objective, explain the incompatibility rather than silently changing it.

Return a ranked critique with code/evidence citations; a keep/delete/replace table; the **single simplest next mechanism** you would back, its derivation and falsifiers; alternatives you refuse and why; and a bounded implementation/validation plan. Distinguish demonstrated failures, plausible hypotheses, and missing evidence. Give each proposed measurement its cheapest adequate instrument and estimated cost, with a stop condition. Test chromosome/cached loci come first; full ladder, both gaps, independent calibration and ruler scores, and VCaP decide a surviving candidate. VCaP has approximate origin-pool truth, not transcript truth.

Do not launch costly runs during a document-only review. If execution is authorized, announce budgets first, use pinned scan and fractional assignments, keep the machine idle for timings, prototype outside main, and do not commit. The desired answer may be “abandon this mechanism.” It must explain how the recommended alternative earns its simplicity and moves the actual release forward.
