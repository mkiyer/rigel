## ④ What this baseline says the message layer must fix

1. The relay's value is **real and concentrated** where the local solve is blind — it is not to be
   turned off (`ROADMAP.md` rank 1: not a blanket default flip).
2. Its harm is **concentrated where the destination has good own evidence**, and grows with gDNA.
3. The suspected mechanism is ②'s unearned precision, not the message's value.
4. ⭐ The two panels AGREE in sign on both halves, which is what licenses developing on the test
   chromosome — but confirm on the ladder before believing any change
   (`TRAPS: a-toy-and-a-panel-can-disagree-in-rank`).
