# Calibration variance over-confidence: adversarial design review

**Status:** decision document for the Rigel team.
**Scope:** the per-node simplex solve's imputation/prior precision model, and four candidate directions for fixing the `τ → 1e7–1e11` over-confidence pathology.
**Anchored to code state** as of `bp_solver.py` HEAD: IF1 (the `_MSG_PSEUDOCOUNT=1.0` Poisson floor, line 537) is **shipped**; the global Poisson-offset prototype (`gprior_vm` keyed on the non-circular `rho_obs`, lines 647–657) is **live in the working tree**; IF2 (query `σ²_bio` at `ρ_src`) is **not in** — `_message` line 534 still queries `predict(rho_dst_cur)`, fed the destination's own snapshot density (`SNG/SNP/SNN[df][i]`) at lines 671/678/683.

---

## 1. Executive summary

### 1.1 The bug, in one paragraph

Calibration deconvolves each node's unspliced mass into `(f₊, f₋, f_g)` by a precision-weighted product of (a) a Beta-Binomial **strand** likelihood whose plus-rate `p = ½ + (κ−½)·t` depends **only** on the tilt `t = f₊−f₋` (never on `f_g` directly), and (b) quadratic imputation/prior penalties `−½τ(f−μ)²`. The imputation precision is `τ_f = τ_ρ·(M/E)²` with `τ_ρ = 1/(σ²_bio + ρ_src/E_src)`. The `(M/E)²` Jacobian is **algebra** — it is the change of variables from a density variance to a fraction variance (`f_g ≡ M_g/M`), and the architecture (§2) blesses it. The pathology is that `σ²_bio` is **under-estimated on a circular or wrong axis**, and the (correct) Jacobian then magnifies a finite density-variance error into a `τ` of `1e7–1e11` that overrides the correct strand likelihood. Single-strand nodes are immune (their strand is sharp in `f_g`: `t = f₊ = 1−f_g`). **AMBIG nodes** — where the strand is flat in `f_g` — take the full hit.

### 1.2 The two traced cases (the acceptance test for every proposal)

- **reg242** (capture, AMBIG exon, balanced `u₊/u₋ = 10065/10523`, oracle `f_g = 0.976`). The strand is genuinely flat in `f_g`. The **global** prior pins `f_g → 0` with `τ_glob ≈ 7.7e6`: its mean `μ_glob = ρ_global·E/M ≈ 0.001` is capture-blind (un-enriched baseline ÷ enriched mass), and `τ_glob = (M/E)²/σ²` re-inflates through the same Jacobian. **The capture leak.**
- **reg231** (zero-gDNA library, AMBIG exon, 99%-positive `u₊/u₋ = 16336/149`, oracle `f_g = 0`). The RNA messages crush `f₊` and `f₋` to 0 with `τ ≈ 1.5e11` (from a self-query: `σ²_bio(ρ_dst)` evaluated at the node's own crushed density, where the monotone curve is smallest), overriding 16,336 positive reads → `f_g = 1.0` **phantom gDNA**.

### 1.3 What the review established (the points of consensus)

Across every lens, three facts held up and should be treated as settled:

1. **The `(M/E)²` Jacobian is not the bug.** It is the definitional density↔fraction normalizer. Removing it is a *units error*. The bug lives entirely in the variance input `σ²_bio` (and the `var_p` Poisson floor).
2. **The status quo respects count-zero-information.** No count votes on composition; counts enter only as strand Fisher precision, imputation Poisson precision, and the blessed Jacobian. The bug is a *variance-honesty* failure, orthogonal to count-zero-info.
3. **reg242 is the global prior; reg231 is the RNA message.** They are two faces of one disease — a density variance under-estimated on the wrong axis — but the fix sites are distinct, and the global fix (the `rho_obs`-keyed width + self-solvable mean) is already prototyped and recovers reg242 (−73%, 202K→54K).

### 1.4 Recommendation

**(a) Short-term stabilization — adopt now, in this order, as one reviewed change:**

1. **Commit the global prototype** (already in the working tree) — it is what actually fixes reg242. Clean up the orphaned `global_gdna_prior` function and its 3 now-stale tests, and add a zero-gDNA test for the new `tau_global = max(…, 1.0)` floor path.
2. **Ship IF2 as the frozen-snapshot variant** — query `σ²_bio` at the *source* density, taken from the **frozen snapshot**, not the live Gauss-Seidel neighbour. This is the message-level twin of the global fix and kills the reg231 phantom *on the stranded case*.
3. **Promote the population-RNA-dispersion floor from "optional follow-up" to part of the fix.** Floor `σ²_bio` at the genuine population RNA dispersion `σ²_RNA,pop` and add a **zero-source gate** (a message with `ρ_src ≈ 0` exports `τ = 0`, not mass-scaled confidence). Without these, IF1+IF2 *do not* fully fix reg231 — the silent-strand / mutual-crush case still reconstitutes `f_g = 1.0` at `τ ≈ M²`.
4. **Cap `τ_f` at the lattice resolution and the strand Fisher information.** A precision finer than one lattice cell (`τ_max ≈ K² ≈ 3600` at `K=60`) is unrepresentable and degenerates the median readout to an argmax; a `τ_f` exceeding `N(2κ−1)²` lets a prior structurally override the intrinsic strand. Cap both. This makes "the count never overrides the strand" a structural invariant rather than an emergent hope, and it neutralizes both bugs even where the variance fix under-performs.

This package keeps every principled component (the quadratic form, the Jacobian, the BB strand likelihood, the spliced floor, the monotone `var~mean`) and changes only the variance *inputs* and adds two structural ceilings. Blast radius is the calibration solve only; downstream EM reads f_g point estimates, so the cost is golden-TSV regeneration + review.

**(b) Long-term architecture — do not commit to a coordinate or currency change yet.** Both Alt A (pseudo-counts) and Alt B (ALR) are *structurally* attractive — they each remove the Jacobian and make the over-confidence bounded-by-construction. But both, on close inspection, **relocate the arbitrary resolution of the AMBIG `f_g` ridge rather than eliminate it**, and Alt B (ALR with gDNA baseline) puts a **coordinate singularity exactly on the zero-gDNA pin** — the dominant operating regime. Neither fixes reg242 by its own mechanism (both need the orthogonal honest-mean global fix). The honest conclusion: the stabilizers achieve the same correctness target at ~two orders of magnitude less blast radius, with no new singularity. **Revisit Alt A's pseudo-count reframing only for the global prior and the spliced floor** (where it cleanly removes the `M²` double-hit with no own-count vote) as a possible *future* unification — not as a wholesale solver rewrite.

## 2. Per-proposal analysis

### 2.1 Status quo (the quadratic-penalty / `var~mean`-Poisson-offset model)

**Steelman recap.** The construction is the *unique* form forced by three prior commitments: count-zero-information (count = power, never a vote), a node-as-product-of-evidence (each non-strand term is a log-concave Gaussian penalty — the max-entropy form of "a message = (estimate, precision)"), and the definitional density↔fraction map. Given those, `τ_f = τ_ρ·(M/E)²` is the only internally consistent precision. The two failures are two under-estimates of one input (`σ²_bio` on the wrong axis), and the framework is the object that *localizes* the bug to that input and supplies the same machine (`MonotoneVarMean.fit_offset` on a non-circular axis) to fix it. Emergent deference (no tuned `I₀`) falls out of inverse-variance fusion; single-strand immunity falls out of the precision arithmetic.

**Objections by severity.**

- **SURVIVES (count-zero-info).** Every count/mass appearance is strand Fisher precision, imputation Poisson precision, or the §2-blessed Jacobian. The phantom is a precision-misestimate, invisible to this lens. The framework is compliant.
- **SURVIVES (impl-cost).** The fix is small and composes; downstream EM reads only f_g masses. (Caveat: the global prototype is uncommitted, leaves a dead-but-tested orphan, and adds a 3rd `var~mean` fit per pass on the known hotspot.)
- **MAJOR (tilt-degeneracy).** At a balanced AMBIG node the strand is **not** flat — with `od > 0` the `−½log var` term makes `L_strand` *decreasing* in `f_g` (a spurious down-pull, peak at `f_g ≈ 0.2–0.33`). Recovering reg242's near-vertex `f_g = 0.976` requires `τ_glob ≈ several hundred` — i.e. the global must near-pin, which is **over-confidence relocated onto a truth-ward mean**, not cured. Confirmed: the live `tau_global` (line 657) carries the same `(M/E)²` (= `1/geom2_global`) Jacobian; the only thing between it and a huge `τ` is `sigma2_n`. The "-73%" win works at reg242 only because `rho_mean·E/M` happens to map near 0.976; a more-enriched-than-average exon gets `μ_glob < truth` and is silently pinned to a population artifact.
- **MAJOR (zero-boundary).** The ALR-singularity attack misfires (the status quo has no `f_g` denominator and handles `f_g=0` as an exact lattice vertex — a genuine advantage over Alt B). But the **f_strand→0 face is a live, uncommitted pathology**: the reg231 self-query. IF1 bounds `τ_ρ ≤ E_src²`, but `×(M/E)²` that ceiling is still `~1e11` at a high-mass exon. Plus dynamic-range fragility: `σ²_bio` is flat-extrapolated at ~71% of captured-exon nodes, so `(extrapolated σ²) × M²` is an `O(1e8)` precision on an unvalidated variance.
- **MAJOR (convergence-solver).** Two findings. (1) The per-pass refit makes the iterated operator non-stationary; the global's *width* is fit on residuals derived from the live `f_g` (lines 651–653) — the *query axis* `rho_obs` is non-circular but the *fitted curve* is circular. The fixed point is set entirely by the self-solvable subset, with no error-correcting feedback from the AMBIG nodes it mis-solves. (2) The lattice cannot represent the `τ` the status quo produces: `τ = 1e7` has σ ≈ 3e-4, ~50× finer than the `1/60` cell, so `_fg_median` returns an argmax (a one-cell delta), not an integral — violating the steelman's own stated validity condition. The sweep burns the full pass budget without meeting the delta criterion (consistent with the "capon uses full 4-pass budget" observation); high-`τ` Gauss-Seidel messages admit L→R/R→L ping-pong.
- **MAJOR (fixes-both-bugs).** Conditional. reg231's `1.5e11` is the *pre-IF1* number; with IF1 the live ceiling is `~M²`, and the phantom is a knife-edge race against the strand's `N(2κ−1)² ≈ 1.6e4`. reg242 is fixed by the global prototype, but per-node recovery depends on `μ_global` extrapolation landing near 0.976; the residual is a +24K overshoot on top of a 54K under-call. The balanced-AMBIG zero-evidence subset (~35K) is genuinely unidentifiable and unfixed.

**VERDICT: adopt-with-fixes.** The framework is principled and the bug is *localized and surgically fixable within it*. But "honest by construction" is overstated: the live code ships a `1e11` precision at the strand-zero pin, recovers reg242 only by relocating over-confidence onto a truth-ward mean, and produces a `τ` regime the lattice cannot represent. The required fixes are the §1.4(a) stabilizer package — they are framework-preserving (touch only the variance input + add two ceilings), not a redesign.

### 2.2 Immediate stabilizers (IF1 + IF2)

**Steelman recap.** Two minimal, local edits to two arithmetic expressions, no new ψ term. **IF1** adds a `c₀=1` pseudocount to the Poisson floor (`V_p = ρ_src/E_src + c₀/E_src²`), making `τ_ρ ≤ E_src²/c₀` *structurally* finite. **IF2** queries `σ²_bio` at the source density `ρ_src` (the value the message claims, non-circular) rather than the destination's own crushed `ρ_dst`. They bound the two summands of `τ_ρ = 1/(σ²_bio + V_p)`: IF2 keeps the first honest, IF1 keeps the second non-zero. Together: `τ → ∞` becomes structurally impossible, and `τ_f ≤ M²/c₀`. Both are corrections of *implementation infidelities to architecture §1.2*, not new heuristics.

**Objections by severity.**

- **SURVIVES (count-zero-info).** `c₀` enters only the Poisson-noise precision channel, never the mean `μ_f`; it can only *lower* a message's confidence (it is a regularizer, not a vote). IF2 changes only the query argument on the reliability machine. Both are §0-clean. (Guardrail: `c₀` must stay inside `pois`/`tau_rho`, never added to `μ_f`'s numerator.)
- **SURVIVES (impl-cost).** IF1 is shipped (zero cost). IF2 is a ~3-token argument swap at line 534 (`rho_dst_cur → rho_src`). Downstream EM is unaffected (reads f_g masses only). The only real cost is golden-TSV regeneration + a Gauss-Seidel determinism subtlety (below).
- **MAJOR (tilt-degeneracy).** IF1+IF2 kill the divergence but do **not** hold `f_g` honestly at a balanced AMBIG node — they relocate the arbitrary resolution to the RNA-message *mean* direction, at `τ ≤ M²`, which the strand cannot defend. Worse: IF2 is over-confident *exactly in the reg242 regime* — at a gDNA-rich capture exon the RNA source is a depleted flank, so `ρ_src` is genuinely low → IF2 queries the *low* (small-σ²) end of the monotone curve → traced `τ_f = 3.4e6`. Same flat strand, same balanced count, opposite truths (reg242 `f_g=0.976` vs a pure-RNA balanced exon `f_g=0`) are indistinguishable to everything except the RNA mean.
- **MAJOR (zero-boundary).** **IF2 is a no-op on a silent strand.** When the antisense RNA is genuinely absent (reg231's `f_neg→0`), `ρ_src(−) ≈ 0`, so `σ²_bio(ρ_src≈0) ≈ 0` — identical to the circular `ρ_dst` query. The axis falls to IF1's ceiling `τ ≤ M²/c₀`, which grows **quadratically in mass** while the defending strand Fisher grows only **linearly**: at reg231 that is `2.7e8` vs `1.6e4`. The phantom persists. The bound is loosest exactly where it must be tightest (the high-mass exons that take the whole hit).
- **MAJOR (convergence-solver).** **IF2-as-specified is not non-circular under Gauss-Seidel.** In the live `_message`, `ρ_src` for an RNA message is recomputed from the *just-updated* `f_pos[src]`/`f_neg[src]` (lines 676/681) — the live in-place neighbour, not the frozen snapshot. So a left neighbour crushed earlier in the L→R pass drives `ρ_src→0` → `σ²_bio→0` → the phantom recurs as a *propagating crush-wave*. The steelman's "use the frozen-snapshot ρ_src" escape is a *third* design (mean from live neighbour, precision-axis from frozen) and is internally inconsistent unless `μ_f` is also computed from the frozen snapshot. Secondary: the median-readout's piecewise-constant map admits 2-cycles; the bare `max|Δ|` test (line 686) silently exhausts `max_passes`.
- **MAJOR (fixes-both-bugs).** IF1+IF2 fix the *favorable* (stranded) reg231 path but **empirically reconstitute the full phantom** (`f_g = 1.000`) whenever both RNA sources are crushed — which is the AMBIG init state (`{0,0,1}`). At `M=16485`, both RNA messages pinned to 0 at `τ = M² ≈ 2.7e8` vs strand Fisher `1.3e4` → the symmetric-pin phantom (which saturates at `τ ≈ 1e4`) wins. reg242 is fixed by the *global* prototype, not by IF1/IF2.

**VERDICT: adopt-with-fixes — but the fixes are mandatory, not optional.** The literal IF1+IF2 pair is *not* sufficient. To actually fix both bugs the change must additionally: (1) use the **frozen-snapshot `ρ_src`** for the σ²_bio query (and for `μ_f`, for consistency — making the message Jacobi-in-content even though the solve is Gauss-Seidel); (2) **floor `σ²_bio` at the population RNA dispersion** `σ²_RNA,pop` (promoted from "optional"); (3) add a **zero-source gate** (`ρ_src ≤ floor ⇒ τ = 0`); and (4) **cap `τ_f` at the lattice resolution and the strand Fisher info**. The headline "holds f_g honestly at AMBIG" claim must be dropped. With the full package, this is the recommended short-term path.

### 2.3 Alternative A — pseudo-count message framework

**Steelman recap.** Express every message and the global prior as *pseudo-fragments* (a Dirichlet pseudo-count `Σ_c C_c·log f_c`, with `C_c = ρ_src·E_dst`) rather than a quadratic penalty on a fraction. Because the real strand evidence is *also* a count-based likelihood, the prior competes with the data in the same currency and the same order in N. The curvature of `C·log f` is *linear* in the pseudo-count (no `M²`), so the destination mass `M` enters only through `E` (a length), to the *first* power. The Jacobian — the sole super-linear-in-N factor — is structurally absent. "16,000 reads beat a 50-count hint" becomes a theorem (`O(N)` vs `O(C_src)`). The spliced floor becomes a *hard +RNA pseudo-count* (the cleanest object in the framework); the global becomes a gDNA-only pseudo-count `ρ_global·E_dst` with **no M** — removing reg242's double-hit at a stroke.

**Objections by severity.**

- **MINOR (count-zero-info).** The spliced and global pseudo-counts are clean (and the global recast genuinely fixes reg242's `M`-double-hit). But the **neighbour-imputation Dirichlet magnitude** `C_g = ρ_src·E_dst = f_g(src)·M_src·(E_dst/E_src)` is *linear in the neighbour's count* and has **no biological ceiling** (unlike the sanctioned `1/(σ²_bio + ρ_src/L_src)`, which caps at `1/σ²_bio`). The steelman's cap `C ≤ N_pseudo` is non-binding in the Poisson regime (`N_pseudo ≈ C_src` there). So a count-scaled magnitude vote reappears at exactly the AMBIG node class the strand-flatness protects. Fixable by binding the cap and querying σ²_bio at `ρ_src`.
- **MAJOR (tilt-degeneracy).** The Dirichlet mode on the balanced ridge is `f_g* = C_g/(C_g + C_p + C_n)` — a *specific, arbitrary* resolution of the unidentifiable ridge, dressed as principled. Traced: reg242 reaches 0.976 only if the gDNA-flank pseudo-count dominates the RNA-flank by ~40:1, which nothing guarantees (sparse flanks → `f_g 0.38`, RNA-rich → `0.07`). And the over-confidence is *relocated*, not eliminated: `N_pseudo ≈ C_src` is `O(thousands)` for a deep flank, so a deep gDNA-rich neighbour produces a *sharp* `f_g` (sd `~1/√ΣC ≈ 0.016`) — as confident-and-arbitrary as the old `τ=7.7e6`.
- **MAJOR (zero-boundary).** The Dirichlet magnitude term `C·log f` **repels every fraction from 0**. It cannot represent the three zero regimes: it relocates the zero-gDNA phantom (a modest `C_g=5` neighbour against light RNA gives `f_g = 0.667`), and makes the capture `f_g≈0.976` pin **structurally unreachable** against any RNA presence (removing `M` destroys the dynamic range needed to express `f_g→1`). And `N_pseudo ∝ ρ_src²` re-imports a *squared-magnitude* factor (in the source density rather than the dest mass) across the 10⁴ RNA range.
- **MAJOR (convergence-solver).** The "16k beats 50" arithmetic is off by 100–1000×: when neighbours agree, `C_pseudo = ρ_src·E_dst ≈ f_g·M ≈ 10,000`, not 50 — the hint is `O(M)` at the very AMBIG-balanced node where the strand can't defend. The cap doesn't bind for gDNA in a uniform-gDNA library (`σ²_bio→0`, the design doc's own §4.4 observation) when `E_dst ≫ E_src` (deep exon, short flank). The circularity relocates to the neighbour's running estimate; the per-pass `var~mean` fixed point is unchanged. So a *linear*-in-`E_dst` over-confidence returns — milder than `M²`, not structurally absent.
- **MAJOR (impl-cost).** "Minimally invasive" is wrong: it must re-home and re-validate the *shipped* global fix (a second `var~mean` fit, not a message), reconcile with the untouched Jeffreys gate (currently mutually-exclusive with the global; additive pseudo-counts would change correct single-strand-node behavior), and — the landmine — `priors.py` is **already a second, independently-tuned pseudo-count/IPR machine** over a different node set, whose factor-1-under-uniform invariant rides on the boundary f_g the reform would perturb. Two coupled pseudo-count systems feeding each other into the EM.
- **MAJOR (fixes-both-bugs).** Fixes reg231 (phantom dies, and the `ρ_src²` prefactor defuses the relocated self-query), but does **not** by itself fix reg242 — that needs the orthogonal global-*mean* redefinition (capture in the mean), which applies equally to the existing quadratic form. The §4 "bounded curvature" theorem is only true *away from the simplex boundary*: at the `_PRIOR_EPS=1e-3` clip, `C=200` gives curvature `~2e8`, so a large pseudo-count can still stiffly pin near a vertex.

**VERDICT: reject as a wholesale solver rewrite; adopt the narrow reframing for two terms.** The all-Dirichlet "ideal form" trips four lenses (it relocates the AMBIG over-confidence to the pseudo-count ratio, repels fractions from 0, and reintroduces a `ρ_src²`/`O(M)` magnitude). But the steelman's strongest correct insight — recasting the **global prior** and the **spliced floor** as pseudo-counts (no `M`, no Jacobian, no own-count vote) — is genuinely clean and removes reg242's double-hit. Carry *that* into the long-term design; do not adopt the neighbour-imputation Dirichlet.

### 2.4 Alternative B — ALR log-ratio space (gDNA baseline)

**Steelman recap.** The over-confidence is a *coordinate-system artifact*. Re-parameterize the simplex by `y₊ = log(f₊/f_g)`, `y₋ = log(f₋/f_g)` (or the ILR rotation `r = log(f₊f₋/f_g²)`, `d = log(f₊/f₋)`). Then (a) `σ²_bio` becomes a genuine *constant* additive dispersion (log-fold-change has roughly constant CV across the 10⁴ range — the property the monotone curve only approximates), and (b) **the `(M/E)²` Jacobian vanishes** because the message is already a prior on a density *ratio* (`M` cancels), so there is no density→fraction conversion to amplify. A crushed RNA fraction is `y→−∞` with finite variance and no precision spike. The flat strand direction becomes the explicit `r` axis. Precision is bounded by `1/σ²_bio,y = O(1)`.

**Objections by severity.**

- **FATAL (zero-boundary).** gDNA-as-denominator puts the coordinate singularity **exactly on the zero-gDNA pin**, the dominant operating regime. As `f_g→0`, both `y→+∞` (the exact truth is at infinity). The global prior `g_global = log(ρ_g/ρ_rna)` is `log(0) = −∞` in a zero-gDNA library — undefined precisely where the phantom must be killed. Box truncation at `y_max=12` makes `f_g=0` structurally unreachable (reg231's point estimate needs `y=13.8`, *outside* the box). And the mandatory change-of-measure `½Σlog f` (needed to keep the simplex integral unbiased) is an **active anti-pin bias**: it penalizes the true `f_g→0` corner by `~2r`, manufacturing a 26% gDNA phantom in a zero-gDNA library at an unanchored balanced AMBIG node — where the *current* linear code returns `f_g=0` *exactly*. The status quo's worst-handled regime over-confidence is independently fixable in place; ALR turns the status quo's *best*-handled, exact regime into a structural singularity.
- **MAJOR (tilt-degeneracy).** ALR does not honestly resolve `f_g` at balanced AMBIG — it relocates the arbitrary resolution from the Jacobian to the prior-mean + r-box midpoint + change-of-measure (no-evidence median `≈0.26`, box-dependent: `[−12,6]→0.69` vs `[−6,12]→0.10`). It is precision-honest, not identification-honest. (The steelman concedes this in §9, so the lens does not *break* it — but the headline "the bug disappears" is overstated.)
- **MAJOR (convergence-solver).** The frozen-snapshot `var~mean` refit **ingests ±∞**: in a stranded/zero-gDNA library, `f_neg≈0` at most nodes → `y=−∞` on nearly every `−`-strand edge → the finite-mask silently drops them → `_constant_offset` fallback fires → the per-pass adaptive refit *degenerates exactly where it's most needed*. The circularity doesn't disappear: either `σ²_bio,y` is a true constant (= the already-measured `--kill-rna-prec` constant-prior regime, 52K→35K, which ALR re-derives, not transcends) or it's queried at the dst's crushed coordinate (identical self-query, new variable). The convergence test goes blind in the tail (fraction-space `Δ` is wildly non-uniform in `r`; a still-drifting node registers as converged).
- **MAJOR (impl-cost).** Not "bounded surgery" — a coordinate rewrite of the entire solver core (lattice + all four evidence terms + `var~mean` + readout estimator + init/locking convention), atomic with no incremental on/off switch (a mixed linear/log solver is incoherent). It touches all four named files plus three test suites plus the golden scalars, and the only end-to-end-validated contract (factor-1-under-uniform, atol=0.02) rides on `_fg_median`, which the re-tiled lattice + change-of-measure weight **silently perturbs** (the "median reparam-equivariance" argument is wrong for a discrete CDF walk).
- **MAJOR (fixes-both-bugs).** Fixes reg231 (phantom dies — the O(1) ceiling defangs the message and the sharp strand wins). Does **not** fix reg242: the single-population-scalar `g_global` is *mixture-diluted* by the un-enriched majority (toy: `f_g ~ 0.05–0.27`, never 0.976), and the scalar width discards the density-adaptivity that the *current* linear global fix relies on for its −73%. **New regression:** the O(1) ceiling that kills reg231 also disarms the global on a *genuinely-zero* balanced-AMBIG node — it can no longer sharply pin `f_g→0` (traced: same info that gives `f_g=0.05` in linear space gives `f_g=0.65` in ALR). ALR trades over-confidence for under-confidence on the same AMBIG class.
- **SURVIVES (count-zero-info).** ALR propagates solved compositions and count-as-power only; no pseudo-count vote. Removing the Jacobian *strengthens* §0 compliance. (Guardrail: the box bounds must be count-independent.)

**VERDICT: reject.** The structural elegance is real (constant log-dispersion; Jacobian-free messages), but gDNA-baseline ALR places a coordinate singularity on the zero-gDNA pin — the *most important* operating regime — where the status quo is currently exact and robust. The change-of-measure self-inflicts an anti-pin bias; the var~mean refit ingests ±∞; it does not fix reg242 and regresses the genuine-zero case; and it is the highest-cost option with the only-validated contract silently perturbed. The fixes that would rescue it (RNA-baseline moves the singularity, not removes it; density-adaptive precision re-imports the Jacobian-flavored term ALR claimed to delete) each dismantle a load-bearing claim. Dominated by the in-place stabilizers on every axis that matters.

## 3. Head-to-head comparison

| Axis | Status quo (+stabilizers) | Alt A: pseudo-count | Alt B: ALR (gDNA baseline) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Count-zero-info** | Clean. Count = power only; Jacobian blessed. (SURVIVES) | Global + spliced clean; **neighbour Dirichlet reintroduces a count-scaled magnitude vote**, uncapped by biology. (MINOR) | Clean; removing Jacobian *strengthens* §0. Guard: count-independent box. (SURVIVES) |

**The unifying read.** All three proposals *bound* the divergence. None *resolves* the AMBIG `f_g` ridge from local evidence — because a balanced count is genuinely equally consistent with pure-gDNA and balanced bidirectional RNA. The status quo + stabilizers is the only option that (a) keeps `f_g=0` exact, (b) localizes the bug to a variance input, and (c) supplies the honest-precision machine already validated on the sibling (global) term. Alt A and Alt B each remove the Jacobian elegantly but pay for it by relocating the arbitrariness and (Alt B) introducing a singularity on the dominant regime.

---

## 4. Decisive open questions

These must be resolved before committing to *any* long-term direction beyond the stabilizers.

1. **Is the balanced-AMBIG `f_g` ridge resolvable from a node-intrinsic signal at all?** Every proposal resolves it by *some* prior (global mean, pseudo-count ratio, box midpoint). The only candidate intrinsic signal that is `f_g`-discriminating at balanced strand is the **gDNA-FL vs RNA-FL fragment-length likelihood** (gDNA fragments have a distinct FL pmf), which the scorer already uses but the calibration strand-mixture does not. **If a node-intrinsic FL term can break the ridge, the entire prior-honesty debate becomes secondary.** This is the highest-value question and is orthogonal to the coordinate/currency choice.

2. **Should the honest answer at a genuinely-degenerate node be a *wide posterior*, not a confident median?** A balanced, zero-evidence AMBIG node is unidentifiable in any coordinate. The architecturally-correct output is a large `var_g` deferring to the downstream per-locus EM (which has spliced/junction evidence the calibrator lacks) — not a confident point estimate. **Decide whether the calibrator's contract should emit and propagate `f_g` uncertainty.** If yes, the "-73%" framing on the balanced subset should be re-reported as a high-variance estimate, and the `_fg_var` readout must be made honest under high `τ` (the lattice one-cell delta currently reports false certainty).

3. **What is the right `τ_f` ceiling, and is a hard cap acceptable?** Capping `τ_f` at `min(K², N(2κ−1)²)` makes "the count never overrides the intrinsic strand" a *structural invariant* and neutralizes both bugs even when the variance estimate is poor. It is the cheapest correct stabilizer. **The open question: is a documented cap a "magic number" violation, or a sanctioned numerical-resolution constant (like `K=60`)?** The team's no-magic-numbers discipline must adjudicate this — but note the cap is *derivable* (lattice resolution + Fisher information are both computed, not tuned).

4. **Can the per-pass refit be made a genuine contraction?** The iterated operator is non-stationary (the `var~mean` and global mean/width are refit from the iterate they then constrain). A formal convergence guarantee requires either a snapshot frozen across the *whole* sweep (not just within a pass) or damping (`f_new ← (1−η)f_old + η·f_solve`). **Decide whether to invest in a contraction proof or accept empirical convergence with an oscillation detector** (the current `max|Δ|` test silently exhausts the budget).

5. **Is the population-RNA-dispersion floor estimable, and how large is `σ²_RNA,pop`?** The stabilizer package leans on it to convert the `M²` bound into something modest (`M²/σ²_RNA,pop`). RNA varies ~10⁴-fold, so it *should* be large — but it must be measured (the analog of the global's self-solvable spread), and it must be a **population** quantity, never per-node count-derived (or it smuggles the count back in as confidence). **Measure it on the cached conditions before relying on it.**

6. **Does the `priors.py` factor-1-under-uniform invariant survive any change to how boundary `f_g` is produced?** This is the one place a calibration-side change reaches the EM (boundary `f_g` → `mass_gdna_left/right` → seam IPR → `gdna_eff_len` → the per-locus Dirichlet split). **Any reform — stabilizer or architecture — must add a test pinning factor-1 with f_g from the new solve** before golden regeneration.

---

## 5. The single recommendation, restated

**Ship the stabilizer package now** (commit the global prototype + frozen-snapshot IF2 + `σ²_RNA,pop` floor + zero-source gate + `τ_f` cap at `min(K², Fisher)`), validate on the three cached conditions via `scripts/debug/dissect_regions.py` and the `calibration-benchmark` net-flow, regenerate goldens with review, and **drop the "honest at balanced AMBIG" claim** — that subset is genuinely unidentifiable and should be handled by an honest wide posterior + downstream EM, or by adding the node-intrinsic FL term (Q1).

**Do not adopt a coordinate (ALR) or currency (full Dirichlet) change.** Both relocate rather than eliminate the AMBIG arbitrariness, neither fixes reg242 by its own mechanism, and ALR introduces a fatal singularity on the zero-gDNA pin. **Carry forward only Alt A's narrow win** — the global prior and spliced floor as `M`-free pseudo-counts — as a candidate future cleanup, gated behind the factor-1 invariant test.
